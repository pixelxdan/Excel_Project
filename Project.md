# Sales Analytics Dashboard
## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Sales Analytics Dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and departmental expenses.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional sales data into actionable business insights using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI calculations, and interactive reports.

### Objectives
- Track daily and monthly sales performance
- Analyze customer contribution to revenue
- Monitor product-wise sales and profitability
- Evaluate departmental expenses
- Identify top-performing customers and products
- Provide management-ready business reports

### Dataset
The project contains:

**Sales Data**
- Invoice Number
- Invoice Date
- Product Details
- Customer Information
- Quantity Sold
- Gross Sales
- Discount %
- Net Sales
- Gross Profit
- Order Status
- Sales Region

### Expense Data
- Expense Type
- Department
- Amount
- Payment Status
- Vendor Details

### Reports Included
**1. Daily Sales Report**  
Tracks:

- Invoice Date
- Quantity Sold
- Gross Sales
- Discount %
- Net Sales
- Gross Profit
  
**2. Monthly Sales Report**
Provides:

- Customer-wise sales summary
- Revenue contribution
- Profitability analysis
  
**3. Customer Report**
Analyzes:

- Customer contribution %
- Customer-wise revenue
- City-wise sales performance
  
**4. Product Report**
Shows:

- Product sales performance
- Top-selling products
- Product profitability

**5. Expense Report**
Tracks:

- Department-wise expenses
- Expense categories
- Paid vs Pending expenses

**6. Dashboard**
Interactive dashboard displaying:

- Total Sales
- Net Sales
- Gross Profit
- Expense Breakdown
- Customer Insights
- Sales Trends
